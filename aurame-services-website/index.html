Who We Are

A Young Engineering Team Focused on Practical Design

Aurame Services Pvt. Ltd. is a design and consultancy firm serving clients in the process, oil & gas, and manufacturing sectors. Our team combines academic engineering knowledge with hands-on design experience to deliver calculations, drawings and reports that meet international codes and client specifications.

From concept sketches to detailed fabrication drawings, we work closely with our clients at every stage of the project to keep designs safe, efficient and easy to manufacture.
