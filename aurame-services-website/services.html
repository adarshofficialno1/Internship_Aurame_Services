What We Offer

Engineering Services Built Around Process Equipment

We provide design calculations, drawings and consultancy support across four core service areas. Each project is handled by a small team so designs stay accurate, well-documented, and easy to review.

Pressure Vessel Design

We design pressure vessels including separators, storage vessels and reactors as per ASME Section VIII Division 1 and other applicable design codes. Our scope covers thickness calculations, nozzle reinforcement checks, support design and preparation of fabrication drawings.

  • Shell and head thickness calculations
  • Nozzle and opening reinforcement checks
  • Saddle / skirt / leg support design
  • General arrangement and fabrication drawings

Heat Exchanger Design

Our team carries out thermal rating and mechanical design of shell & tube and plate heat exchangers for a range of process duties. We work with client-specified process data to arrive at a design that meets duty, pressure drop, and code requirements.

  • Thermal rating and sizing calculations
  • TEMA and ASME-based mechanical design
  • Tube bundle and baffle layout
  • Datasheets and fabrication drawings

Mechanical Engineering

Beyond pressure equipment, we support clients with general mechanical design requirements including static equipment sizing, piping support design and basic structural checks for equipment platforms and skids.

  • Static equipment sizing support
  • Piping support and skid design assistance
  • Equipment platform and walkway checks
  • General mechanical calculation reports

Engineering Consultancy

We offer independent design review, documentation support and general technical consultancy for plant projects. This includes reviewing vendor drawings, checking calculations and helping clients prepare technical specifications.

  • Third-party design and drawing review
  • Technical specification preparation
  • Code compliance checks
  • Project documentation support
