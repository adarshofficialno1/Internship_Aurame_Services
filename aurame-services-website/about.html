Company Overview

Engineering Design Support You Can Rely On

Aurame Services Pvt. Ltd. was set up to provide dependable design and consultancy support to process and manufacturing industries. Our engineers work on pressure equipment, heat exchangers and general mechanical systems, translating client requirements into safe, code-compliant designs.

We keep our processes simple and transparent — clear communication, documented calculations, and drawings that are easy for fabricators and clients to follow. This approach has helped us build long-term working relationships with clients across the process and industrial sectors.
